The map of the sleep

You are asleep, and you are trying to wake: reach END. The fortune teller gives every move, and the map carries you through five chapters. On every night stone you must hide before the night is over.

The Returner is not hunting you. It is the old, protective part of her that keeps going back to what once felt safe, and it learns the ways you trust.

  1. The fortune teller. Say a word, pick a number, lift its flap: how far you move, and a question.
  2. The map. Gold rings lift you ahead, red rings send you back. You must stop on every ★ night stone.
  3. The streets. Four turns to reach a refuge. The streets you know are lit and quick; new ones are dark and slow. From the second night, the Returner walks the streets you know.

One player plays the whole game. The next player starts a new game after the questions. v1, for two players, is at v1/.

Places from the game's own renders. Think aloud while you play.
